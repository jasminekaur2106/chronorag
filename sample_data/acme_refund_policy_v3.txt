Acme Store Refund Policy

Refund window

Customers may request a refund within 21 days of purchase.

Digital goods

Digital goods are not eligible for refunds once downloaded or accessed.

Shipping costs

We refund the original shipping cost whenever the return is caused by an error on our side.

Gift cards

Gift cards are non-refundable.

Contact

Refund requests are handled through the help center chat or by email at support@acme.example.
