Acme Store Refund Policy

Refund window

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of purchase.

Digital goods

Digital goods are not eligible for refunds once downloaded or accessed.

Shipping costs

Original shipping costs are refunded when the return is caused by our error.

Gift cards

Gift cards are non-refundable.

Contact

Refund requests are handled by email at support@acme.example.
